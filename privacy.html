Privacy Policy

Last updated: 20 September 2026

This policy covers the private applications operated by the owner of this site, including a personal instance of rclone that synchronises the owner's own files with Google Drive.

Scope

These applications are single-user and private. They are not offered to the public, they have no user registration, and they are not used to process anyone else's data.

What is accessed

  • Files and folders in the owner's own Google Drive account, through the Google Drive API, after the owner has granted consent.
  • An OAuth token issued by Google, stored locally on hardware owned by the site owner.

How it is used

Access is used solely to copy files between the owner's own server and the owner's own Google Drive account. Nothing is analysed, profiled, sold, or shared with third parties.

Where data is stored

Files remain in the owner's Google Drive account and on the owner's own hardware. The OAuth token is stored on that hardware with access limited to the system administrator. No copies are transmitted anywhere else.

Revoking access

Access can be withdrawn at any time from the Google Account permissions page, which immediately invalidates the token.

Cookies and analytics

This page sets no cookies and loads no analytics or tracking scripts.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent through github.com/justajazz.
